# Social Media Content Post

Your 24/7 creative assistant that turns simple ideas into professional social media posts. It handles the design and writing, but never posts anything without your final "Thumbs Up" via Telegram.

[Watch in Action](https://meherazhossain.com/#)

## Overview

Automates the entire process from idea to multi-platform post — including content creation, a human approval loop, and auto-publishing.

## Technical Logic & Workflow

1. **Start Your Way** — Launch a new post by simply texting or sending a voice message with your idea to Telegram, or adding a line to your Excel sheet for bulk planning.
2. **Instant AI Design** — The system automatically creates high-quality images based on your niche and branding.
3. **Perfect Captions & Tags** — It writes the post text and finds the best hashtags so you don't have to.
4. **You Have the Final Say** — The AI sends a preview to your Telegram. It waits for you to say 'Yes' before posting anything.
5. **Smart 'Try Again' Loop** — Not a fan of the first draft? Just tap 'No,' and the AI instantly generates a new design for you.
6. **One-Click Blast** — Once approved, your post goes live on Instagram, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, and Facebook all at once.
7. **Auto-Organized Library** — Every approved photo is saved to your Google Drive, and every caption is stored in your Google Sheet automatically, keeping all your content organized in one safe place.
8. **Total Remote Control** — Manage your entire social media presence directly from your phone via Telegram messages.

## Adaptability

Built for your business — whether Consultants, E-commerce, or Coaching, the logic adapts to your specific style.

## Nodes / Tools Used

- Telegram (trigger + approval interface)
- Google Sheets (bulk input + caption storage)
- AI image generation
- AI text/caption + hashtag generation
- Google Drive (media library)
- Instagram / LinkedIn / Facebook (multi-platform publish)

## Setup

1. Create a Telegram bot and add the token
2. Connect Google Sheets and Google Drive credentials
3. Add API credentials for each social platform (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook)
4. Set the Telegram chat ID for the approval loop
